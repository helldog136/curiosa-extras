# Blog

Articles with an image, a summary and Markdown content. Add as many blogs as you want.

Base module of Curiosa (Curiosa License 1.0). It is turned on from the admin, under **Features**; its settings are on its own page.

## Display

Each blog is shown as cards, as a list, as chips or as codes (setting "Display" of the blog, advanced mode).

- **List**: one full-width card per article, with the title and the summary cut with an ellipsis. The whole row opens the article.
- **Search field**: a "Show a search field" setting in the General tab of each blog. On by default in list display. It filters while typing, ignoring capitals and accents, over the title, the summary and the tags, and over all pages when the blog has several. Needs Curiosa 0.6.18 or later.
