# Sponsor ticker overlay (OBS) — Curiosa community module

A "lower third" banner: a card with a diagonal cut that slides in from the right every *N* seconds with the sponsor's name,
promo code, a short summary and a **QR code**. Three looks (neon, cinema, broadcast), a free accent colour, and a "New" badge for 14 days.

**Feeding it** (instance page → *Data sources*):

| Topic | Source |
|---|---|
| `sponsor.card` | The *Sponsors* module (name, text, link, code, logo, date), filterable by tag |
| `core.entry` | Any list of entries that have a code or a link (for example the core *Promo codes* module) |

The overlay knows none of these modules. QR codes are generated by the core (`ctx.api.qr`).

**OBS**: a browser source on `https://your-site/overlays/<key>` (1920×1080). Advanced setting "Developer mode":
`?dev=1` (appears after 0.5 s, every 4 s) and `?seed=42` (same order every time), to test without waiting.

**Long texts and small sources**: the title shrinks (down to 18 px), then wraps to 2 lines before being cut; the summary keeps
2 lines; the QR caption shows the domain name without "www." (cut in the middle only above 22 characters); the card grows if the text needs it.
In a browser source smaller than the card (800×200, 600×150, 340×100…), it scales down to stay fully visible, with a margin. At 1920×1080 with short texts, nothing changes.

**In an overlay page** (the "Overlays" section of Curiosa 0.5.0 or later): "Add an element" offers **Sponsor banner**. The banner fills its frame at scale (natural size 720 × 180) and follows the page's common style (light or dark text, background, corners); the look (neon, cinema, broadcast), the accent, the frequency and the duration stay the module's. Option: **Show the QR code** (never more than the module setting). In test mode the banner stays on screen, with a sample card when there is no sponsor. Your current OBS address does not change.

**How it enters and leaves** (version 1.2.0, under "More options", core 0.6.0): by default the card slides in from the right and leaves the same way, exactly as before. Pick another way for each direction: slide from the left, right, top or bottom, fade, jiggle (a short bouncy entrance; on the way out, a wobble then a fade) or pop (zoom), or none. "Same as how it enters" (the default for leaving) plays the entrance backwards. The length of the movement goes from 0.2 to 2 seconds. The two choices exist both as module settings (for the standalone overlay) and as options of the element (default: like the module settings), so two banners in one page can move differently. For people who ask their device for less motion, any movement becomes a short fade. The code that plays the movement is written once in `scripts/overlay-transition.mjs` and copied to `transition.mjs` (`node scripts/gen-transition.mjs`).
