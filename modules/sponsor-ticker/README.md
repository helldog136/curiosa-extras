# Sponsor ticker overlay (OBS) — Curiosa community module

A "lower third" banner: a card with a diagonal cut that slides in from the right every *N* seconds with the sponsor's name,
promo code, a short summary and a **QR code**. Three looks (neon, cinema, broadcast), a free accent colour, and a "New" badge for 14 days.

**Feeding it** (instance page → *Data sources*):

| Topic | Source |
|---|---|
| `sponsor.card` | The *Sponsors* module (name, text, link, code, logo, date), filterable by tag |
| `core.entry` | Any list of entries that have a code or a link (for example the core *Promo codes* module) |

The overlay knows none of these modules. QR codes are generated by the core (`ctx.api.qr`). A QR code leads to the page of YOUR site that presents the sponsor (never straight to the referral link, which is one tap away on that page); when the public address of the site is not set, no QR code is shown. It fills the full height of the banner: the white plate is flush with the top and bottom edges, the white margin is 2 modules, every module is a whole number of pixels (the banner grows by a few pixels to make that possible, never beyond its frame), and the banner stays still while a QR code is shown. The tests decode the QR code (`tests/sponsor-ticker-qr-decode.test.mjs`).

**OBS**: a browser source on `https://your-site/overlays/<key>` (1920×1080). Advanced setting "Developer mode":
`?dev=1` (appears after 0.5 s, every 4 s) and `?seed=42` (same order every time), to test without waiting.

**Long texts and small sources**: the title shrinks (down to 18 px), then wraps to 2 lines before being cut; the summary keeps
2 lines; the card grows if the text needs it.
In a browser source smaller than the card (800×200, 600×150, 340×100…), it scales down to stay fully visible, with a margin. At 1920×1080 with short texts, nothing changes.

**In an overlay page** (the "Overlays" section of Curiosa 0.5.0 or later): "Add an element" offers **Sponsor banner**. The banner fills its frame at scale (natural size 720 × 180) and follows the page's common style (light or dark text, background, corners); the look (neon, cinema, broadcast), the accent, the frequency and the duration stay the module's. Option: **Show the QR code** (never more than the module setting). In test mode the banner stays on screen, with a sample card when there is no sponsor. Your current OBS address does not change.

**How it enters and leaves** (version 1.2.0, under "More options", core 0.6.0): by default the card slides in from the right and leaves the same way, exactly as before. Pick another way for each direction: slide from the left, right, top or bottom, fade, jiggle (a short bouncy entrance; on the way out, a wobble then a fade) or pop (zoom), or none. "Same as how it enters" (the default for leaving) plays the entrance backwards. The length of the movement goes from 0.2 to 2 seconds. The two choices exist both as module settings (for the standalone overlay) and as options of the element (default: like the module settings), so two banners in one page can move differently. With reduced motion on the device, the default entrance and exit become a fade of the same length (no sliding, no loops); the other movements become a short fade. The code that plays the movement is written once in `scripts/overlay-transition.mjs` and copied to `transition.mjs` (`node scripts/gen-transition.mjs`).

**Which sponsor is on screen** (version 1.6.0): your site decides, from its own clock, and the page sends nothing back. Time is cut into slots as long as the display period (never shorter than the display time plus 2 seconds); each slot has one sponsor, shown during its first seconds (the display time). Sponsors come in rounds: in each round every sponsor appears once, in an order that changes from round to round (shuffled from the round number), and the first of a round is never the last of the previous one. The page gets the server time with the list of sponsors, so it shows the planned sponsor at the planned moment even if the clock of the OBS computer is wrong. In the test page (`ov-demo`) and in the developer mode (`?dev=1`, `?seed=N`) the choice stays random, as before. The code that decides is written once in `rotation.mjs` and used by the site and copied into the page.

The module provides the optional topic `home.info` (`sponsor-ticker.<key>`, label "Sponsor on screen", value the name of the scheduled sponsor during its display time, `null` between two displays or without sponsor): the Home Assistant connector adds it to what Home Assistant fetches. Because the site computes it, it is right even when the OBS page is closed or was loaded late. Nothing else reads it, and nothing is needed to use the banner without it.

**Motion** (version 1.7.0): the default entrance is fast with a 4 % overshoot and stops with zero velocity; the exit starts softly and ends crisply (its own curve, no longer the entrance replayed); the logo, text and QR code follow the plate by about 2 frames each; the float and the shine start once the card has landed and stop while the banner is hidden; the neon look now shows its glow (a still, blurred copy of the card's shape placed behind it, so it costs one paint per card and nothing per frame), while the blur of the standalone page, which never showed, is gone. Audit and measurements: `docs/SPONSOR-MOTION-AUDIT.md`. With reduced motion on the streaming PC, the core still removes the fade on skinned overlays (a core matter): the card then appears and leaves at the right moments without a fade.
