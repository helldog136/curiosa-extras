## What it does

A retro 3D maze for OBS. The walker wanders through the corridors and stops in front of posters made from your site's content.

- Add the overlay as a browser source in OBS.
- Choose the size of the maze and its colours.
- Pick wall and floor textures.
- Set the walking speed and the time a poster stays in focus.

## Why you will like it

- A playful, unusual background for a quiet moment on stream.
- It shows your own content as posters.
