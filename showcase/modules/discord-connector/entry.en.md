## What it does

Links your site to your Discord server with a bot, once, for everything that talks to Discord. A guided setup takes about five minutes.

- Follow the guided setup and register your bot.
- Add the bot to your server with one link.
- Let other modules post and open private threads through the same bot.

## Why you will like it

- You connect once, not once per module.
- The bot only asks for the permissions it needs.
