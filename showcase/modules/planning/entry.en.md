## What it does

Show your upcoming streams in the look you choose. They come from your Google Calendar or from what you write by hand.

- Pick a look: list, zigzag, week, timeline or posters.
- Place blocks on your home page: the next streams, the next stream, or the next days in a compact form.
- Show the week schedule and the next stream in OBS.
- Tune the page: days shown, time format, first day of the week, game covers and partners' photos.

## Why you will like it

- Your audience always knows when you are on.
- No retyping: the calendar you already use feeds the page.
- Game covers and partners' photos make each slot easy to spot.
