## What it does

An agenda of your events and trainings: upcoming dates, a monthly calendar and a detailed page for each date.

- Filter upcoming dates by type, or search them.
- Show a monthly calendar.
- Give each date its own page: time in the visitor's time zone, programme, speakers, sign-up and video.
- Let visitors add a date to their calendar and look back at past events.
- Show the next dates on your home page.

## Why you will like it

- Visitors see the time where they live.
- One agenda for talks, trainings, lives and meetings.
- Past events stay available as a look back.

## Good to know

- No name or e-mail is asked, and no cookie is set.
- A YouTube video loads only after a click.
- Your AI assistant can list events and create or change them.
- What it adds to your site: blocks for your home page; its own page; access for your AI assistant.
