## What it does

A GitHub button in your site header, plus your followers.

- Paste the link of your profile and the button appears in the header.
- Add one account per profile if you have several.
- See your followers on a Statistics tab.
- Switch the figures off if you prefer.

## Why you will like it

- Your audience finds your GitHub in one click.
- The figures are public: there is nothing to connect.

## Good to know

- Only a secure address from the right site is accepted.
- Tracking parameters are removed from your link.
- Nothing is shown until the profile is set.
- If GitHub does not answer, the module says so in plain words and shows no made-up number.
