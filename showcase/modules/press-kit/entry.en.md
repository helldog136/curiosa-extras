## What it does

A public page that presents your identity to press and partners. Presentation, facts, logos, colours, fonts, contact.

- Use your site's own colours and fonts by default.
- Write a presentation, a one-line summary and the main facts.
- Add your logos, also for dark backgrounds.
- Show a press contact name and, if you wish, its e-mail.

## Why you will like it

- Journalists and partners find everything in one place.
- Fill it in yourself, or ask an assistant.
