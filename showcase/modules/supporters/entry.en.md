## What it does

Thank your supporters from a patronage platform. The public page shows only the nicknames they accepted.

- Bring your supporters in from the platform, or import a file.
- Send each person a personal link to agree to appear.
- Read each nickname before it appears, if you wish.
- Choose the public label of each tier.
- File supporters in Contacts.

## Why you will like it

- Nobody appears without agreeing.
- You thank people without exposing amounts.

## Good to know

- The page never shows an amount, a real name or an e-mail.
- Signing up to the platform is not agreement: people agree on their personal link.
- Your AI assistant can list supporters and approve or hide a nickname.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; access for your AI assistant.
