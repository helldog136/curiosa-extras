## What it does

A small, discreet character in a corner of your site. It says hello, gives a tip or announces something new, then steps aside.

- Use its own drawing, or yours.
- Write the sentences it says.
- Choose the pages and the corner where it appears.
- Let it announce news from other modules.
- Show it in OBS.

## Why you will like it

- It adds personality without being pushy.
- You control how often it speaks.

## Good to know

- It collects nothing and sends nothing; the browser only remembers visitors' choices.
- No cookie is used.
- Your AI assistant can list its sentences and add one.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; access for your AI assistant.
