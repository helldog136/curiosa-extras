## What it does

A blank content module: you choose its display, its fields and its behaviour yourself.

- Create a collection for anything: projects, recipes, resources.
- Choose how it is displayed.
- Define the fields each entry has.

## Why you will like it

- No need to wait for a module made for your idea.
- You decide the behaviour yourself.
