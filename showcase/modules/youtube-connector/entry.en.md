## What it does

Keeps your site's YouTube access in one place. It reads your channel counts and the chat of your live.

- Save your YouTube access once.
- Fill in the Google client details for your account.
- Set the daily allowance your site may use.
- Let your site answer in your live chat, if you wish.

## Why you will like it

- Every YouTube module shares one connection.
- You stay within a daily limit that you choose.
