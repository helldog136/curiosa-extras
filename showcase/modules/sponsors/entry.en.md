## What it does

Shows your sponsors and their promo codes on your site, with the partnership notice that the rules ask for.

- Add a sponsor with its page and promo code.
- Show a partnership notice above the list and on each sponsor page.
- Write the notice in each language.

## Why you will like it

- The notice is built in, so it is not forgotten.
- One page per sponsor, with a short link.
