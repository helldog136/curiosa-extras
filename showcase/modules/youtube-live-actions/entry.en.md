## What it does

When someone supports your YouTube live, with a Super Chat or a membership, an action runs at your place.

- Write rules that link a donation to an action.
- Read the chat during your live.
- Let your moderators stop everything from the chat.
- Show donations in the alerts overlay.
- Simulate a donation to test a rule.

## Why you will like it

- Support from your audience has a visible effect.
- You can cut everything at once.

## Good to know

- A log keeps the YouTube name, amount and result for your team only (50 lines at most).
- A message's text is never written to disk.
- Your AI assistant can read the status and the journal, and simulate an action.
- What it adds to your site: access for your AI assistant.
