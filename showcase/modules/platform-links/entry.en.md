## What it does

Add any other platform as an icon next to your social networks. An optional notice page (18+) can open first.

- Add as many platform links as you need.
- Show a notice page before sensitive platforms open.
- Write the title and the message of that notice.
- Tell the platform where the visitor comes from, if you wish.

## Why you will like it

- Platforms without their own module still get an icon.
- Visitors are warned before an adult platform opens.
