## What it does

Collect reviews from people who like what you do. Every review waits for your approval before it appears.

- Share a “Leave a review” link.
- Approve, refuse, edit and favourite reviews.
- Show reviews as a grid or as slow scrolling, with optional stars.
- Show favourite reviews, the average rating or an invitation on your home page.
- Show community reviews in OBS.

## Why you will like it

- Nothing shows without your approval.
- Kind words are easy to collect and to show.
- Stars and an average rating are optional.

## Good to know

- The e-mail address, if you ask for it, stays private and erases itself.
- Your AI assistant can list reviews, summarise them and approve or refuse them.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; blocks for your home page; access for your AI assistant; e-mail notifications.
