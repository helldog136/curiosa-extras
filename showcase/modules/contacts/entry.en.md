## What it does

Your private address book: people and organisations, their accounts on the networks, categories, notes and a photo.

- Keep each person once, with their accounts on Twitch, YouTube, Bluesky and more.
- Sort people into categories.
- Search by name, handle or organisation, and find duplicates.
- Import and export your address book.

## Why you will like it

- Other modules link to a person instead of copying them.
- You retype nothing when you thank, raid or partner with someone.
- It stays private: only your team sees it.
