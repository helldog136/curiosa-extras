## What it does

Your site receives what happens on your channel. It keeps a journal you can read, search and replay.

- Follows, subscriptions, resubs, gifted subs, bits and raids.
- The start and end of your stream, and hype trains.
- A list with a search, a filter by type, and a test button for each type.
- Switch each type on or off. The page tells you when Twitch's permission is needed.
- The journal is shared with your other modules, such as the alerts in OBS.

## Why you will like it

- Nothing is lost when your site restarts: each event is stored once, even if Twitch sends it twice.
- You see at once if your site stopped listening, and what to do.
- Only public names and amounts are kept, for 30 days by default.

## How it looks

The admin page shows the journal first, with a status line and a Check now button.

## Good to know

- Needs: [Twitch connector](entry:twitch-connector), [My Twitch channel](entry:twitch-channel).
- Your site needs a public secure address (https) so that Twitch can reach it.
- The first time, Twitch asks you to accept a few more permissions (bits, hype trains).
- Your AI assistant can read the journal, check the listening and replay an alert.
- What it adds to your site: access for your AI assistant.
