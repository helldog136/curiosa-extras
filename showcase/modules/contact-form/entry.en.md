## What it does

Adds a contact form under a page of your choice. Each message becomes a contact to review in your address book.

- Pick the page that shows the form.
- Receive each message in your address book.
- Get an e-mail for each new message, if you wish.

## Why you will like it

- Visitors can write to you without showing your e-mail address.
- Messages never get lost in a mailbox.

## Works well with

- [Contacts](entry:contacts)
- [Pages](entry:pages)

## Good to know

- Needs: [Contacts](entry:contacts).
- The name, e-mail address and message are kept in your team's private address book, only to answer.
- They are neither published nor passed on.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; blocks for your home page; e-mail notifications.
