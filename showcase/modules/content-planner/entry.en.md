## What it does

Your private notebook for videos, articles and posts. Ideas, work in progress, published: with steps to tick off.

- Note an idea with just a title.
- Move it from idea to in progress, published or archived.
- Tick off the steps of each idea, and set a target date.
- Download the notebook as a CSV file.

## Why you will like it

- You never lose an idea.
- You see what is next at a glance.
