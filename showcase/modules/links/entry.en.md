## What it does

A list of links with icons: your sites, your projects, your favourite pages. Social networks have their own modules.

- Add a link with a title and an icon.
- Keep several lists if you have several channels.

## Why you will like it

- One page that gathers all your links.
- Easy to share in a bio.

## Works well with

- [Blog](entry:blog)
- [Pages](entry:pages)

## Good to know

- Button links for social networks come from the social network modules.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; blocks for your home page.
