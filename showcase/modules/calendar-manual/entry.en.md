## What it does

Write your streams here, with day, time, games and weekly repeats. Your site shows them, with no Google account to set up.

- Add a stream with its day, time and games.
- Repeat a stream every week.
- Show the streams on the schedule and in the chat bot.
- Set the time zone of the times you type.

## Why you will like it

- Nothing to connect: you just type.
- A good start if you do not use an online calendar.
