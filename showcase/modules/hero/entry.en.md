## What it does

A big title and an intro for the top of your home page. You place its section where you want it.

- Write a title, an intro and a small line above the title.
- Add a button and choose where it leads.
- Use a video, with an image shown before playing.
- Let visitors turn the sound on, and show your logo.

## Why you will like it

- Visitors understand who you are in the first second.
- One block, and your home page has a front door.

## Works well with

- [Blog](entry:blog)

## Good to know

- The banner is a section: you place it where you want on your home page.
- What it adds to your site: blocks for your home page.
