## What it does

A thank-you page for your community: cards with photos, and one person in the spotlight each month. Names stay in Contacts.

- Group people by Contacts category, up to six groups.
- Write a short message for each person.
- Choose the person in the spotlight.
- Show the thank-yous in OBS.

## Why you will like it

- Thanking people in public takes you only a few clicks.
- People already in your address book appear without retyping.

## Good to know

- Needs: [Contacts](entry:contacts).
- A card in the “Do not show” category never appears.
- Nothing is collected about your visitors.
- Your AI assistant can write messages and choose the person in the spotlight.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; access for your AI assistant.
