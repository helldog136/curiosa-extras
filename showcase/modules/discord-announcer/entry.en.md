## What it does

Announces your new content on a Discord channel, with no bot or account to link. It also tells your server when you go live.

- Paste the sending address of a Discord channel.
- Announce your new content there.
- Announce your lives on Twitch and YouTube, with a text you write.
- Choose how long to wait between two live announcements.

## Why you will like it

- Your Discord hears about it first.
- There is no bot to install or to feed.
