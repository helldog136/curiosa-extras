## What it does

A public showcase of your members, creators or partners, and of your team. Visitors search, filter and open a profile.

- Choose which Contacts categories are public.
- Let visitors search by name and filter by category.
- Write a short profile for each person.
- Let visitors print or download the list.
- Hide or show a person again in one click.

## Why you will like it

- The people come from Contacts: nothing to retype.
- The category “Do not show” always wins.

## Good to know

- Needs: [Contacts](entry:contacts).
- No cookie, no form and no visitor address kept.
- Only what you choose to publish appears: name, categories, links and photo.
- Your AI assistant can search the directory and read a profile.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; access for your AI assistant.
