## What it does

Finds the cover of your video games, even when one source is down: RAWG first, then Steam, then Twitch.

- Find covers with no code needed.
- Fix a wrong cover in a few clicks.
- Keep a copy of each cover on your own site.
- Choose when a kept cover is renewed.

## Why you will like it

- Your schedule and your lists look good with no effort.
- Visitors never contact RAWG, Twitch or Steam themselves.

## Good to know

- A free RAWG access code is optional.
- The game title is sent to RAWG, to Steam and to Twitch to find the cover.
- Your AI assistant can list covers to check and pick the right game.
- What it adds to your site: access for your AI assistant.
