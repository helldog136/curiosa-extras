## What it does

A small game once a day, to give people a reason to come back. One question, a few answers, an explanation.

- Write questions, import them or turn on a ready-made pack.
- Show one question a day, the same for everyone.
- Keep a streak of days in each visitor's browser, with no account.
- Turn on a weekly ranking, as an option.
- Show the state of the game on your home page.

## Why you will like it

- It brings people back tomorrow.
- Your mascot can invite people to play.
- You choose the order of the questions.

## Good to know

- No account and no cookie: the streak and scores stay in the visitor's browser.
- Your AI assistant can list the questions and add one.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; blocks for your home page; its own page; access for your AI assistant.
