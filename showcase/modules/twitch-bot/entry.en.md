## What it does

Your site answers the commands typed in your Twitch chat. It also runs timers, channel point rewards and an emergency stop.

- Create commands that your site answers in the chat.
- Set timers: commands that repeat by themselves.
- Create and change channel point rewards from your admin.
- Cut everything with an emergency stop that your moderators can also trigger from the chat.
- Answer only while you are live, if you prefer.

## Why you will like it

- Everything is managed from your own site.
- A reward can trigger an action at home with Home Assistant.
- If you allow it, moderators manage commands from the chat.
