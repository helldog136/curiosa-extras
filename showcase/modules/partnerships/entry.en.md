## What it does

Your private partnership pipeline: the brands you approach, their status, follow-ups and a journal. It has no public page.

- Add a brand and follow it from first contact to published.
- Keep a journal of each exchange.
- Link the people from your address book.
- Get a follow-up reminder after a number of days without news.
- Import a list of partners.

## Why you will like it

- You see where every deal stands.
- Nothing is forgotten after the first message.
