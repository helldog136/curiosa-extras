## What it does

Reads your Google Calendar, or any online calendar, so your site can show your streams without retyping anything.

- Paste the secret address of your calendar once.
- Show your streams on the schedule and in the chat bot.
- Set the calendar's time zone, if it needs one.
- Choose how many minutes pass between two reads of the calendar.

## Why you will like it

- You keep planning in the calendar you already use.
- Changes in your calendar reach your site by themselves.

## Works well with

- [Stream schedule](entry:planning)

- [Hand-written streams](entry:calendar-manual)

## Good to know

- The calendar address is a secret: keep it private.
- A guide in the admin shows where to find it in Google Calendar.
- A file to download teaches an AI assistant to fill your calendar the way your site expects.
