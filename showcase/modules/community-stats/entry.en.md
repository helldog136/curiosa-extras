## What it does

Keeps the numbers of your networks, such as followers, paying followers, posts and views, and how they evolve.

- Note your numbers automatically, at the pace you choose.
- Add by hand the numbers a network does not give.
- Keep the history for as long as you decide.
- Show counters in OBS.

## Why you will like it

- You see your growth over time.
- Your AI assistant can answer “how many followers did I gain this month?”.
