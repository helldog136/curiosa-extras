## What it does

Announce a release, a launch, a birthday or a convention. Visitors see the time left, and the time where they live.

- Create a countdown with a date and an end message.
- Let visitors add it to their calendar.
- Show a featured countdown, or the upcoming ones, on your home page.
- Put a countdown on screen in OBS, with the place, size and colour you choose.

## Why you will like it

- Your audience builds up to the big day with you.
- The same countdown works on your site and on stream.
- Each visitor sees the date in their own time zone.

## Good to know

- Your AI assistant can list, create and change countdowns.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; blocks for your home page; its own page; an address for OBS; access for your AI assistant.
