## What it does

Let your audience send you ideas. One field, one button, then a list of the ideas you keep, each with a support button.

- Review each idea: publish it, refuse it or merge it with a duplicate.
- Let people support the ideas they like.
- Set limits: ideas per hour, length of an idea.
- Publish without review, if you trust your audience.

## Why you will like it

- You hear what people want without reading a long thread.
- Duplicates are merged, so support adds up.

## Good to know

- An idea keeps its text and a nickname if given, and shows only after review.
- No real name or e-mail address is asked.
- Your AI assistant can list ideas and change their status.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; access for your AI assistant.
