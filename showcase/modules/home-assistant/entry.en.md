## What it does

Links your channel points to your home: lights, a message read aloud, and actions you name. A big “Cut everything” button stops it all.

- Pair your Home Assistant with your site.
- Let a reward switch a light or read a message aloud.
- Refuse numbers and unwanted words in messages read aloud.
- Set the maximum length of a message and the wait between two messages.
- Send a test to your home from the admin.

## Why you will like it

- Your audience can act on your room, within the limits you set.
- You can stop everything at once.

## Good to know

- A log shows the username, the action and the result, for your team only (100 lines, 7 days).
- A refused message is not kept, only the reason.
- What it adds to your site: access for your AI assistant.
