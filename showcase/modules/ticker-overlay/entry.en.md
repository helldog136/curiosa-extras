## What it does

A transparent browser source for OBS that rotates cards from your site, such as promo codes, articles and announcements.

- Add the overlay as a browser source in OBS.
- Choose how many seconds each card stays.
- Choose the text colour, the card colour and the position.

## Why you will like it

- Your site's content works for you during the live.
- The background is transparent, so it blends into your scene.

## Works well with

- [Promo codes](entry:codes)
- [Blog](entry:blog)
- [Sponsor ticker overlay (OBS)](entry:sponsor-ticker)

## Good to know

- The cards come from the content of your site.
- What it adds to your site: an address for OBS.
