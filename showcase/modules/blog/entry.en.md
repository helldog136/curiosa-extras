## What it does

Articles with an image, a summary and simply formatted content. You can add as many blogs as you want.

- Write articles with an image and a summary.
- Run several blogs, each with its own page.
- Place a blog on your home page.
- Show a blog as a list of cards, with a search field that filters while you type.

## Why you will like it

- A place for your news that belongs to you.
- Each blog can have its own theme and its own readers.
