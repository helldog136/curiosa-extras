## What it does

Let your audience suggest what to play. A public list shows each game with its status: proposed, planned, played.

- Offer a suggestion form to visitors.
- Let people vote to play a game again.
- Add games yourself, one by one or a whole list.
- Find covers automatically.
- Show the top suggested games in OBS.

## Why you will like it

- Your audience feels part of your choices.
- You see at a glance what people want.
- A suggested game can become a planned stream.

## Good to know

- A suggestion shows the game's name, a nickname if given, and a note, on the public page.
- A small cookie remembers a visitor's votes for the month.
- Your AI assistant can list suggestions, add games and change their status.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; access for your AI assistant; game covers from RAWG.
