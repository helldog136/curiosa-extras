## What it does

Ask your audience a question and watch the answers come in as bars. One vote per person, with no account to create.

- Write a question and its choices, or start a quick poll in one step.
- Show the current poll on your home page.
- Put the live results on screen in OBS, as bars that update by themselves.

## Why you will like it

- People vote in a few seconds, without signing up.
- The results are easy to read for you and for your audience.
- You choose how long a quick poll lasts.

## Good to know

- Votes are counted per person, without asking for a name.
- Visitors' IP addresses are never saved.
- Your AI assistant can list polls, read results, create and close them.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; blocks for your home page; its own page; an address for OBS; access for your AI assistant.
