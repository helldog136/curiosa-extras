## What it does

Shows a “live now” banner and your player on your home page while you are live.

- Show the Twitch player only while you are live, or always.
- Choose which player wins if you are live on both.
- Choose the YouTube player.
- Name the channels to announce.

## Why you will like it

- Visitors can join your live from your home page.
- The banner disappears when you stop.

## Good to know

- The module collects nothing itself.
- When a player shows, the visitor's browser contacts Twitch or YouTube, which see their address.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; blocks for your home page.
