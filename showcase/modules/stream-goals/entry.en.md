## What it does

Show a goal and a subathon on your overlay pages. They move live with your Twitch events.

- A goal bar towards a number: your followers, your paid subs, or a number you keep by hand.
- A subathon countdown: each sub, bits or follow adds time, by the rules you choose.
- The time added is written on screen, such as "+5:00".
- Pause the subathon, add or remove time by hand, and test both before going live.

## Why you will like it

- Your audience sees each follow or sub count, a few seconds after it happens.
- The look follows the skin of your overlay page: change the skin, the bar changes too.
- Nothing is counted twice, even with two overlay pages open.

## Good to know

- It works without any Twitch module: set the value and the time by hand.
- Your AI assistant can read the goal and the subathon; changing them is off until you allow it.
- What it adds to your site: two elements for your overlay pages; a "Goals" page in "Live"; access for your AI assistant.
