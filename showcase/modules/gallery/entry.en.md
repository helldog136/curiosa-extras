## What it does

Photo albums for your site: event recaps, portfolio or behind the scenes. Visitors open them in a full-screen viewer.

- Create albums and add photos.
- Publish or take down an album whenever you wish.
- Show the latest albums on your home page.
- Handle requests from people who ask for a photo to be removed.

## Why you will like it

- People who appear in a photo can ask for its removal in a clear way.
- Your photos are stored on your own site.
- You can show a consent notice about people in the photos.

## Good to know

- No tracking cookie is used.
- Your AI assistant can list albums, create one, add photos by address and hide a photo.
- What it adds to your site: blocks for your home page; its own page; access for your AI assistant; e-mail notifications; storage for the files you upload.
