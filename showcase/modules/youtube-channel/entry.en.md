## What it does

Showcases your YouTube channel. Its most popular recent video, your videos in the site's feed, your counts.

- Feature your most popular recent video.
- Choose how recent a video must be to be featured.
- Allow or refuse Shorts.
- Follow your channel's statistics.

## Why you will like it

- Your best video is on your home page without effort.
- New videos reach your site's feed by themselves.

- The module collects nothing itself.
- A video preview loads its picture from YouTube, which then sees the visitor's address.
- What it adds to your site: blocks for your home page.
