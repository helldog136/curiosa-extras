## What it does

Free-form pages at the root of your site, such as /about or /contact.

- Write a page with simply formatted content.
- Start from a ready-made template: it creates a draft that you edit before publishing.
- Give each page its own address.

## Why you will like it

- A home for everything that is not an article.
- Nothing is published without you: templates start as drafts.

## Works well with

- [Blog](entry:blog)
- [Contact form](entry:contact-form)
- [Legal pages](entry:legal-pages)

## Good to know

- Each template begins with a line saying it is an example text.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; blocks for your home page.
