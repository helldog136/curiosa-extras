## What it does

Links your site to Twitch once, for everything that touches Twitch. A guided setup takes about five minutes.

- Follow the guided setup, with a Copy button for each value.
- Fill in the two fields of your Twitch application.
- Let every Twitch module use the same connection.

## Why you will like it

- You connect once, not once per module.
- The other Twitch modules just work after that.
