## What it does

A lower-third banner for OBS. From time to time it shows one sponsor, with its promo code and a QR code.

- Add the overlay as a browser source in OBS.
- Choose the seconds between two appearances and the seconds on screen.
- Show or hide the QR code.
- Pick the look and the accent colour.
- Pick how the banner enters and leaves: from any side, fading, jiggling or popping.

## Why you will like it

- Sponsors get seen without a long ad.
- The QR code leads viewers straight to the offer.
