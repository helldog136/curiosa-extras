## What it does

A clear queue for anyone who creates on request: open, full or closed, with your packages and a short form.

- Switch between open, full and closed, with the free places counted.
- Show your packages with indicative prices.
- Collect requests with a short form.
- Give each customer a private tracking link.
- Keep a simple list of requests for yourself.

## Why you will like it

- Customers know at once if you take requests.
- No payment is handled: you stay in charge.
- Customers can see their place in the queue, if you allow it.

## Works well with

- [Contacts](entry:contacts)

## Good to know

- Seeing customers' e-mail addresses and downloading the list are separate rights.
- Only your team reads a request, and the address is never published.
- Your AI assistant can read the queue and change its state.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; access for your AI assistant; e-mail notifications; blocks for your home page.
