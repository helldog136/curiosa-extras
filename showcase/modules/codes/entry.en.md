## What it does

Partner offers with a code, a link and an expiry date.

- Add an offer with its code and its link.
- Set an expiry date for each offer.
- Place the offers on a page of your site.

## Why you will like it

- Your audience finds every code in one place.
- Each offer keeps its code, its link and its date together.

## Works well with

- [Sponsors](entry:sponsors)
- [Sponsor ticker overlay (OBS)](entry:sponsor-ticker)
- [Ticker overlay (OBS)](entry:ticker-overlay)

## Good to know

- The same offers can rotate on screen with the Ticker overlay.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; blocks for your home page.
