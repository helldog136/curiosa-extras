## What it does

A page of questions and answers, grouped by category, that open and close. Visitors can search and ask their own question.

- Group your answers by category.
- Let visitors search the page.
- Offer an “It helped” or “Not really” button under each answer.
- Let visitors send a question, then answer it from the Received tab.
- Show the most helpful questions on your home page.

## Why you will like it

- You answer once instead of repeating yourself in the chat.
- You see which answers help.
- Visitors find an answer without waiting for you.

## Good to know

- A question sent by a visitor keeps only its text: no name, no e-mail, no address.
- Received questions are erased after 90 days at most.
- Your AI assistant can read received questions and draft an answer for you to review.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; blocks for your home page; its own page; access for your AI assistant; e-mail notifications.
