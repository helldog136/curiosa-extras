## What it does

The base of your stream interactions: a nickname per person with no account, a phone remote and a prize draw.

- Give each person a nickname, without any account.
- Run your live from your phone: start, stop or move to the next item.
- Mute someone, clear a room and read the journal.
- Run a prize draw: open it, close it and pick winners.
- Show the prize draw in OBS.

## Why you will like it

- Nobody has to sign up to take part.
- The remote fits in your pocket.
- Other modules, such as Polls, share the same nicknames.

## Good to know

- You can forbid words in nicknames and set their length.
- An inactive person is erased after the number of days you choose.
- Visitors' IP addresses are never saved.
- Your AI assistant can read rooms and the journal, and run the prize draw.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; its own page; an address for OBS; access for your AI assistant.
