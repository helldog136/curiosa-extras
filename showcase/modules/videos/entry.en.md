## What it does

A page of YouTube videos without hosting anything: a grid, categories, search, series to watch in order and sharing.

- Add a video by its address.
- Sort videos into categories and series.
- Let visitors search and share.
- Publish or hide a video or a series.
- Show the latest videos on your home page.

## Why you will like it

- You host nothing: the videos stay on YouTube.
- Series let people watch in the right order.

## Works well with

- [Photo galleries](entry:gallery)
- [Events](entry:events)
- [YouTube channel](entry:youtube-channel)

## Good to know

- No cookie is used and nothing is kept about visitors.
- Their browser contacts YouTube only after a click.
- Your AI assistant can list the library and add a video by address.
- What it adds to your site: its own page; blocks for your home page; access for your AI assistant.
