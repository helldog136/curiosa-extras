## What it does

Showcases your Twitch channel: a header button, your live status and your latest clips. Once connected, it adds your followers.

- Show your live status and latest clips.
- Count your followers and paying subscribers.
- Measure your lives: average and peak viewers, hours streamed and watched.
- See followers gained per game.
- Show followers and a “Live” badge in OBS.
- Change the OBS skin by itself for each game.

## Why you will like it

- You see which games help your channel grow.
- Visitors see at once if you are live.
