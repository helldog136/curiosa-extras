## What it does

Answer a few simple questions and get your legal notice, privacy page and cookies page. They come in two languages.

- Choose your country: Belgium, France, Switzerland, Canada or elsewhere.
- Preview the texts before publishing.
- Publish or withdraw the pages.
- Add links to them at the bottom of every page.
- Get a reminder to review your texts after a number of months.

## Why you will like it

- You do not start from a blank page.
- The pages are in two languages.
