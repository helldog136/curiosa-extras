## What it does

A list of useful places, with filters by type, a search and an OpenStreetMap map. Studios, good addresses, events.

- Add places and sort them by type.
- Let visitors search and filter the list.
- Show a map only when the visitor asks for it.
- Offer a “Near me” button.
- Show places that come from other modules, such as events.

## Why you will like it

- The list works without any outside map.
- Visitors choose when to load the map.

## Works well with

- [Events](entry:events)
- [Directory](entry:directory)

## Good to know

- The map loads from OpenStreetMap only after a click, which then sees the visitor's address.
- “Near me” asks the browser for the position only after a click.
- Your AI assistant can list places and add one.
- What it adds to your site: small additions to your pages; its own page; access for your AI assistant; the visitor's position, only after a click.
